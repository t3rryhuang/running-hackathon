# CheckIn — brand

## What this thing is

CheckIn phones you (or texts you) once a day and asks how you're doing. It
remembers. It is not a productivity tool and it should not look like one — no
dashboards, no streaks, no gamified habit-tracking. The nearest emotional
reference is a friend who actually calls.

## Positioning in one line

> A friend who rings to ask how your day went, and remembers what you said.

## Personality

| We are | We are not |
|---|---|
| Warm, unhurried, plainspoken | Chirpy, motivational, exclamation-heavy |
| Honest ("nothing good to suggest right now") | Eager to please |
| Quiet at night; the product lives in the evening | Bright, high-stimulus, notification-hungry |
| Human-scale — one question at a time | Dense, dashboard-shaped |

## Voice and copy rules

- Second person, contractions, short sentences. "I'll ring you now."
- One question per screen and per message. Never stack two asks.
- Never apologise for a bad recommendation — don't make one. If there's no good
  fit, say so and ask what they'd actually turn up to.
- No fake enthusiasm about someone's day. "That sounds rough" beats "You've got
  this!"
- Never claim knowledge we don't have about the person. Interests are only what
  they told us.

## Mark

`static/brand/checkin-mark.png` — an original asset generated for this project
(no third-party or proprietary artwork was used). A soft speech bubble in warm
apricot with three cream bars inside: simultaneously a message, a voice
waveform, and lines in a journal — the three things the product is.

Sizes committed: 512 (source), 192 (PWA/apple-touch), 64 and 32 (favicon).

Usage:
- Keep clear space of at least 25% of the mark's width on every side.
- Only on the ink background or on pure white. Never on the apricot.
- Do not recolour, rotate, outline, or add effects.

## Wordmark

"CheckIn" — one word, capital C, capital I. Set in Fraunces 9pt-optical at
weight 600 with `-0.01em` tracking. Never "Check In", "Check-in" or "CHECKIN".

## Colour in one glance

Ink `#12151C`, apricot `#FF9A6B`, cream `#F4EDE4`. Full palette with contrast
ratios in [DESIGN-SYSTEM.md](DESIGN-SYSTEM.md).

The blue accent used in v1 and v2 is retired: every hackathon dashboard on the
day is blue-on-black, and blue reads as "software tool" rather than "someone
checking in on you".
